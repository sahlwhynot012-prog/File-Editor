Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

In short: PDF Studio works inside your browser. Your PDFs are not uploaded to our servers, because we don't run any. If you choose to sign in with Google, the app talks directly from your browser to your own Google Drive, only for files you open or save with it.

1. Who we are

PDF Studio is a free PDF editor for writing, drawing, signing and sharing PDFs, available at this website. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

2. Information we handle

We do not have a database, we do not receive copies of your documents, and we do not create user accounts of our own.

3. Signing in with Google

Sign-in is optional. When you choose it, PDF Studio uses Google Identity Services and asks for a Google Drive permission (drive.file, or drive when the built-in file browser is on). That permission lets the app see and change only the files you open with it or that it creates, not the rest of your Drive.

If you use PDF Studio's built-in file browser, it instead asks for the broader Google Drive permission so it can list your files and let you open, save and share them. Those files are read by your browser only to show them to you and to do what you ask. They are never sent to us or any third party.

The access token Google gives your browser is used only to call Google's APIs directly from your browser. It isn't sent to us or to anyone else. You can sign out at any time from the account menu, and you can revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

PDF Studio's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We use Google user data only to provide the features you ask for in the app. We do not sell it, use it for advertising, transfer it to others, or let people read it.

4. Sharing

If you use Share, the app changes the sharing settings of that file in your Google Drive (private, specific people, your organization, or anyone with the link). That happens in your Drive and under Google's rules. Nothing is shared unless you do it.

5. Cookies, analytics and ads

We don't use advertising, tracking or analytics scripts. We don't set tracking cookies. The app stores your preferences and drafts in your browser's local storage. The site's host (Vercel) may keep standard server logs, such as IP address and request time, for security and operations.

6. Third parties

  • Google – sign-in and Drive, only if you use them.
  • Vercel – hosts the website files.
  • Google Fonts / CDN libraries – may load fonts or open-source libraries, which can see your IP address when loaded.

7. Your choices and deleting your data

  • Clear drafts and recents any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.
  • Sign out from the account menu, and revoke access in your Google Account.
  • Files saved to Google Drive stay in your Drive until you delete them there.

8. Children

PDF Studio is not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above. Continued use after a change means you accept the updated policy.
